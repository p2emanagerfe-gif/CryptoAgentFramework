## Executive summary

Gigaverse is a real, revenue-generating on-chain roguelike on Abstract (the Pudgy Penguins/Igloo Inc. L2) — 75K+ paid users, $4.5M+ cumulative revenue, a $2.3M raise led by 1confirmation, and a fresh publishing deal with YGG Play (June 2026). It has **no live token**, an active small-cap NFT layer, and is mid-pivot into a bigger open-world sequel ("Gigaverse Online"). The opportunity is real but time-sensitive and currently under-verified on the data side: our own on-chain check found the most recent reliable trend data is ~3.5 months stale and was declining week-over-week as of April 2026. Treat this as a **promising, not yet confirmed** opportunity — the recommended first move costs no capital.

## What we found

**Player/Market Research (High-Medium confidence):** Gigaverse launched in early 2025 as a simple on-chain combat toy and organically grew to 75,000+ paid users and $4.5M+ revenue before taking any outside capital — genuinely unusual in web3 gaming, where most titles buy growth. Studio GLHF ships fast with no fixed roadmap; team and backers (1confirmation) are credible. It's functioning as an early flagship/anchor consumer app for Abstract, using Abstract Global Wallet as its login. The June 2026 YGG Play publishing deal is the first sign of the game reaching beyond Abstract-native users. The August 2026 "Gigaverse Online" announcement is a genuine sequel-style pivot to an open-world social RPG with a seasonal economy — new ground floor, timing unclear ("a few months out").

**On-chain Analytics (Medium confidence, explicitly stale):** Dune has a full decoded schema for Gigaverse on Abstract, but key tables stopped updating in Feb–May 2025 (likely decoder lag, not a real activity halt), and the freshest usable marketplace data runs only to 2026-04-28 — about 3.5 months old. That last verified window showed ~10-12K weekly listings and ~14-16K weekly fills, but trending down week-over-week. A live CoinGecko snapshot shows the ROMs NFT collection (10K supply, 2,676 holders) with a 24h volume spike of +139.8%, floor ~$94. **Bottom line from that agent: "durable-but-decelerating growth, not a pure speculative spike" — but the most recent quarter's real trend is currently unverifiable with available tools and needs a live re-check.**

**Regulatory (High-Medium confidence):** No live tradeable token. GIGABIT is an in-game currency, explicitly framed by the team as "a rewards engine, not a cryptocurrency," not exchange-listed, with a built-in spread (100 GIGABIT = $1 in, $0.80 out). A points system is widely speculated — community-driven, unconfirmed — to become a future airdrop. No public compliance/jurisdiction statements, no disclosed security audit, no KYC. Abstract itself is run by Igloo Inc. (Pudgy Penguins' parent), whose PENGU token already has SEC ETF-filing visibility — raises the parent company's regulatory profile generally, though it's not a direct Gigaverse red flag today.
